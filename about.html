About us

Built to make online tasks easier.

COCOA TOOLS exists to help people solve everyday digital tasks with less friction and more confidence. We believe free tools should be fast, professional, and genuinely useful across education, productivity, design, SEO, and development.

Our platform is designed around clean UX, fewer distractions, and meaningful functionality. We focus on real utility, secure client-side processing, and accessible interfaces that work on mobile as well as desktop.

We are mindful of AdSense compliance and creator responsibilities. That means no misleading interfaces, no fake buttons, no deceptive ad placement, and no content built only to display ads. We prioritize user value first.
